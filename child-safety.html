Child Safety & Protection Standards – Lynq

Lynq is committed to maintaining a safe and secure communication environment for all users. We strictly prohibit the creation, sharing, or distribution of any form of child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or child sexual exploitation. Such content is not tolerated under any circumstances.

Users may report safety concerns directly through the Lynq application. All reports are reviewed promptly, and when necessary, escalated to the appropriate regional or national authorities in accordance with applicable laws and reporting obligations.

Lynq implements internal measures to prevent misuse of the platform and supports global efforts to protect children online.

For inquiries related to child safety, compliance, or reporting procedures, please contact: information@celyx.net
