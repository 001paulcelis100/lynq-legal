Privacy Policy – Lynq

Last Updated: May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Lynq collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use our messaging application.

1. Data Collection

We collect the following information to provide our real-time translation and messaging services:

  • Account & Profile Data: Your display name, email address, preferred language, Lynq ID, and optional profile photo.
  • Parental Contact Information: For eligible minor users (ages 13–17), we also collect a parent or guardian's email address for supervision and safety restrictions.
  • Messages & Safety Reports: Text messages sent in peer, group, or global chats, as well as user reports or block actions submitted for community safety.

2. How We Use Data

Your data is used to:

  • Facilitate real-time translated messaging across languages.
  • Link you with peers via your Lynq ID or email address.
  • Ensure user and child safety via age-based restrictions, reporting, and blocking tools.

3. Data Sharing & Storage

We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties. Messages are processed via secure automated translation APIs and stored on encrypted Google Firebase servers. Data is only disclosed if required by applicable law or to respond to valid safety and child-protection reports.

4. User Rights & Account Deletion

You can permanently delete your Lynq account and all associated personal data at any time directly inside the app via Profile settings.

If you no longer have the app installed, you may also request complete account and data deletion via the web by emailing 001paulcelis100@gmail.com from your registered email address. Deletion requests are processed promptly.
